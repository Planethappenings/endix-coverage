Method And Notes

How We Measured

The numbers

  • Public data from October 13 to November 12, 2026, pulled from YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook.
  • Average views per video, after removing each channel's two highest and two lowest videos. The same rule applies to the benchmark.
  • Short-form packages are one short published on YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels and Facebook Reels. Estimated views add up each platform's average.
  • Spark Ads range is based on 2026 TikTok benchmarks of USD 10 to 15 per 1,000 impressions in the US and UK.

Worth knowing

  • Figures are 30-day averages. Single videos can land above or below.
  • Short-form and long-form views are different kinds of attention. The package is built for reach and frequency, with long-form for depth.
  • Facebook reports rounded play counts, so Facebook figures are approximate.
  • Final Spark Ads delivery is confirmed once the campaign is set up in TikTok Ads Manager.
