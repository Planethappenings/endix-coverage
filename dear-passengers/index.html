A Fallback Plan Built In

Spark Ads promote the creator's own post, not a separate ad. They also work as a safety net in case view behavior is different than expected.

  1. Post organically first. All placements go live as normal creator posts.
  2. Check after 48 hours. Every post is measured against its expected views.
  3. Fallback if views are lower than expected. The Spark Ads budget goes behind the posts that are below target to lift them up.
  4. Boost if views are higher than expected. The budget goes behind the winners to push them even further.
  5. Report on every view. Creators share Spark authorization codes, and results come straight from TikTok Ads Manager.

All figures are estimates based on each creator's plain 30-day averages and 2026 TikTok ad benchmarks. Actual results will vary and are not guaranteed. The Spark Ads budget is an example and can be set to any amount.
