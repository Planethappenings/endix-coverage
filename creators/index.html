What is Endix

A real game expo, without the plane ticket.

Endix happens twice a year. Brands, studios and publishers build booths in one shared open world, using their own game assets. Watch premieres, play demos and talk to the developers in voice chat. It's free, it's social, and every booth is a new thing to show your chat.

Bring your community

Don’t just stream it. Bring your viewers in.

Endix is a shared world, so your chat can be there with you, not just watching.

Invite your chat in

Endix is free on Steam and Epic, so anyone watching can download it and join you in minutes. Drop the link in chat.

Meet up in the world

Pick a booth or plaza as your community’s meeting spot and fast travel there together. Your viewers become your crew.

Party up and talk

Form a party with viewers and use voice chat to talk in real time while you explore, react and play.

Race your viewers

Take on the challenges together and compare times on the leaderboards. Streamer vs. chat, live.

Emotes and photo moments

Dance, emote and line up for group shots in photo mode. Instant thumbnails and community memories.

Win together

Raffles and giveaways run all weekend, so every draw is a moment you and your viewers share.
